How the game is played
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out onto your start square.
  • Every 6 gives you another roll, as many times in a row as you roll it.
  • Land on an opponent to send them back to their yard, and roll again.
  • Starred squares and coloured start squares are safe. Nobody can be captured there.
  • Go once around the board, then up your coloured lane to the centre.
  • You need the exact roll to reach the centre. Getting a token home earns another roll.
  • First to bring all four tokens home wins. Play continues for the other places.
  • When only one move is possible, it's made for you.
